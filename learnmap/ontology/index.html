Dataset release		A versioned publication boundary that groups learning-map resources, release status, coverage gaps, and rights metadata.

Curriculum		A named and versioned educational programme whose standards and organizing structure apply to a stated jurisdiction and school level.

Grade band		A controlled grouping of school grades used as the intended learning span for curriculum resources.
lm:Subject#Subject
Subject		A curriculum-recognized field of study.
lm:LearningDomain#Learning domain
Learning domain		An organizing area within a subject or curriculum.
lm:AchievementStandard#Achievement standard
Achievement standard		A source-anchored curriculum expectation identified by an official or repository-maintained standard code.equivalentClass core:AchievementStandard
lm:LearningTopic#Learning topic
Learning topic		A repository-authored, teachable unit of learning aligned to one or more achievement standards.equivalentClass core:LearningTopic
lm:LearningCluster#Learning cluster
Learning cluster		A navigational grouping that contains one or more learning topics under a documented coverage policy.equivalentClass core:LearningCluster
lm:EvidenceCriterion#Evidence criterion
Evidence criterion		A learner-observable condition used to judge whether a learning topic has been demonstrated.equivalentClass core:EvidenceCriterion
lm:AssessmentPrompt#Assessment prompt

lm:SourceLocator#Source locator

lm:VerificationRecord#Verification record
