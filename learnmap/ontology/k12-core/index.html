learnmap/ontology/k12-core

K-12 Korean Curriculum Learning Map Core

한국 K-12 학습지도 코어 온톨로지

Classes, properties, and concept schemes shared by the Korean elementary and secondary learning maps. Repository ontologies keep their own IRIs and align to this core.
코어는 어휘만 정의한다. 인스턴스(ABox)는 각 저장소가 자기 네임스페이스로 발행한다.

클래스 (11)

용어레이블정의관계
core:AchievementStandard#성취기준
Achievement standard		A source-anchored curriculum expectation identified by an official standard code.
core:LearningTopic#학습 주제
Learning topic		A repository-authored teachable unit aligned to one or more achievement standards.
core:LearningCluster#학습 묶음
Learning cluster		A navigational grouping of learning topics under a documented coverage policy.
core:EvidenceCriterion#증거 준거
Evidence criterion		A learner-observable condition used to judge whether a topic has been demonstrated.
core:AssessmentPrompt#평가 과제
Assessment prompt		A prompt or task that elicits evidence relevant to a learning topic.
core:SourceDocument#출처 문서
Source document		A document or artifact cited as evidence for curriculum identity, codes, mappings, or provenance.
core:SourceLocator#출처 위치
Source locator		A structured location inside or alongside a source document. The locator kind selects which fields apply.
core:VerificationRecord#검증 기록
Verification record		A dated or status-bearing record of what was checked, by which method, and with what limitations.
core:CoverageGap#커버리지 갭
Coverage gap		An explicit completeness, calibration, reconciliation, or review gap retained for release evaluation.
core:LearningRelation#학습 관계
Learning relation		The common superclass of the two repositories' n-ary prerequisite and recommended-order records. A relation is model-relative and never a universal learning-order fact.
core:StandardTopicAlignment#성취기준-주제 정합
Standard-topic alignment		The n-ary record for a topic-to-standard alignment and all of its qualifiers.

core:facet-application#적용
application		Applying learning to problems and life situations.
core:facet-inquiry#탐구
inquiry		Investigating, gathering data, and reasoning from evidence.
core:facet-communication#의사소통

core:facet-reflection#성찰
reflection		Reflecting on, valuing, and transferring learning.
core:facet-core#핵심
core		The whole achievement standard rather than one facet of it.
