01 / Studio

From creative experiments
to real-world practice.

DEXA is a creative technology studio led by DECK (Yook Dae Geun), a media artist, creator and educator based in Korea.

The practice moves between AI-generated media, site-specific installations, interactive experiences, and creative direction. Alongside this work, DECK develops AI agent and Model Context Protocol (MCP) workflows, knowledge-management systems, and practical AI education for the workplace.

The common thread is turning technical possibilities into experiences, tools and ways of working that people can use.
